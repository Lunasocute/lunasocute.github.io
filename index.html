Catmull-Clark Subdivision

A mesh editor in C++, Qt, and OpenGL, built on a hand-written half-edge data structure. It loads polygon meshes from OBJ files, edits vertices and faces, and smooths any closed mesh with Catmull-Clark subdivision. Each level adds face points and edge points, moves the original vertices, and rebuilds every face as quads. Four levels take the cow from 5,804 faces to 1,114,368, in about 6.2 seconds of subdivision time in a debug build, with time growing almost in step with mesh size.

Noise and Post-Process Shaders

A Qt and OpenGL viewer that switches shaders live on a 3D character. Surface shaders include Blinn-Phong, matcap materials, and a vertex shader that morphs the model toward a cube. Each frame can then pass through a screen-space effect from an off-screen framebuffer: Sobel edges, Gaussian blur, a rippled glass made from Worley noise, or, for extra credit, an animated fog made from domain-warped fBM.

FK/IK Walking Character

An animation toolkit in C++ with a native Unity plugin. Forward kinematics plays BVH motion capture across a joint hierarchy, and three inverse kinematics solvers (analytic limb, CCD, and pseudo-inverse) pose the skeleton toward its targets. A guide joint carries each walk cycle on toward the clicked target instead of snapping back to the origin. Foot IK reads the terrain’s height and normal under each foot, adjusts the hips, re-solves the legs, and aligns the feet to the slope.

Saliency-Aware pruning for 3D Gaussian Splatting

A post-training compression pipeline for 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) models that removes background noise while preserving foreground objects. The system renders training views from a trained gsplat checkpoint, segments regions with Segment Anything (SAM), groups Gaussians into geometric–semantic clusters via constrained union-find, and scores each cluster with a multi-view visibility heuristic (G*).

Style-Conditioned Diffusion on WikiArt

Developed a lightweight, parameter-efficient adapter for Stable Diffusion v1.5 to perform zero-shot artistic style transfer on the WikiArt dataset.
